Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before posting.

Is posting a buying requirement free?

This page is designed for free requirement submission. Confirm the final platform policy before publishing.

What information should I include?

Include the product name, specifications, quantity, budget if available, and delivery city. Clear details help suppliers respond accurately.

Can I attach product images or specifications?

Yes. The form supports up to five PDF or image files. Live storage requires backend integration.

What happens after I submit?

In the intended production flow, your account is verified, the request is saved for review, and relevant suppliers can be notified once it is approved.
